# BHARAT 3D — Interactive Geographic Intelligence Platform

> **Tagline:** Explore India's Geography. Understand Its People. Discover Its Landscape.  
> **Application Type:** Interactive 3D GIS + Civilizational Atlas + Socio-Demographic Visualization + Educational Platform

---

## 🌟 Overview & Architecture

**Bharat 3D** is a premium, cinematic 3D geographic intelligence platform and civilizational atlas for the Republic of India. It combines hardware-accelerated 3D terrain elevation, authentic administrative boundaries, topological river networks with animated flow vectors, pedological soil classifications, 13 historical dynastic eras with an interactive timeline, 18 ASI-verified archaeological sites, ancient trade routes and maritime networks, 28 national highway/expressway corridors, an 8-pillar multi-decadal Environmental Change Explorer with a dedicated Year Comparison Tool, BIS seismic hazard zones, and official socio-demographic statistics (Census of India Primary Census Abstract & C-01 Religion tables).

### Core Principle: Zero AI Assumption of Geographic Facts
In strict accordance with the master specification, **no geographical, demographic, religious, historical, or geological fact is assumed or generated by AI**. Every data point displayed has an authentic, traceable provenance from sovereign Government of India portals or audited global scientific institutions.

## 🚀 How to Run Bharat 3D

### Method 1: One-Click Launch (Windows)
Double-click `start-bharat3d.bat` in the project root. It opens the browser and starts the dev server.

### Method 2: Manual Terminal Execution
```powershell
cd bharat3d-app
pnpm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) in your browser.

### Method 3: Production Build & High-Performance Preview
```powershell
cd bharat3d-app
pnpm run build
pnpm run preview
```

## 🗺️ Key Features Implemented (13 Thematic & Analytical Modules)

1. **Hardware-Accelerated 3D Terrain & Relief:**
   - Full 3D pitch ($0^\circ$ to $85^\circ$) and bearing rotation using AWS / NASA Terrarium RGB DEM elevation tiles.
   - Dynamic terrain relief exaggeration slider ($0.5\times$ to $3.5\times$).
   - Instant 2D / 3D projection toggle with smooth camera transitions.

2. **Historical Atlas & Time Machine (13 Dynastic Eras):**
   - Docked bottom timeline scrubber with auto-play, era steps, and era scrubbing.
   - Covers Indus Valley, Mahajanapadas, Maurya, Gupta, Delhi Sultanate, Mughal, Maratha, British 1909, 1947 Partition, 1956 States Reorganisation, 2000 States, 2014 Telangana, and Present Day.
   - Labeled as approximate scholarly reconstructions for antiquity.

3. **Ancient Civilizations & Archaeological Explorer (ASI):**
   - 18 verified monumental archaeological excavations documented by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI).
   - Category color coding: Indus Valley Settlements (teal), Ancient Universities (amber), Buddhist Monasteries (violet), Rock-Cut Temples (rose), and Ancient Ports (sky blue).
   - In-depth dossiers covering excavation history, key finds, and UNESCO status.

4. **Historical Trade Routes, Maritime Networks & Railway Development:**
   - 27 curated historical features across 7 foundations:
     - *Ancient Trade Networks*: Uttarapatha (2,500 km), Dakshinapatha (2,100 km), Central Caravan Route (1,100 km).
     - *Indian Ocean Maritime Routes*: Western Arabian Sea Monsoon Circuit (3,800 km), Bay of Bengal Suvarnabhumi Network (3,400 km).
     - *Silk Road Connections*: Karakoram–Ladakh Feeder (950 km), Gandhara–Bactria Link (850 km), Sikkim–Chumbi Valley Route (780 km).
     - *Spice Trade Routes*: Malabar Coast 'Black Gold' Pepper Route (850 km), Coromandel Spice & Textile Corridor (750 km).
     - *Ancient Ports & Emporia*: 10 Monumental Seaports (Lothal, Barygaza, Muziris, Arikamedu, Tamralipti, Sopara, Poompuhar, Korkai, Kalyan, Nelcynda) with glowing halos and coordinate dossiers.
     - *Colonial Trade Networks*: EIC Gangetic Factory Corridor (1,350 km), Bombay Cotton & Opium Artery (820 km), Coromandel Calico Corridor (680 km).
     - *Historical Railway Development (1853–1905)*: GIPR 1853 (Asia's first line), East Indian Railway 1854, Madras Railway 1856, Imperial Trunk Grid (Dalhousie plan) with railway cross-tie styling.
   - Interactive category sub-toggles, active pillar counter (`7/7`), "Select All" / "Clear", and dynamic active legend.

5. **Environmental Change Explorer & Multi-Decadal Year Comparison Tool (8 Foundations, 42 Features):**
   - Official multi-decadal environmental tracking grounded in audited sovereign records:
     - *Forest-Cover Changes*: FSI ISFR decadal assessments (2001, 2011, 2021) tracking Western Ghats, Sunderbans, and Northeast forest canopy shifts.
     - *Glacier Retreat*: SAC-ISRO & Geological Survey of India benchmarks (1965 to 2022) for Gangotri (Gaumukh), Siachen Glacier, Chhota Shigri, and Zanskar.
     - *Urban Expansion*: NRSC Decadal LULC (1991 to 2021) measuring built-up land growth for Delhi NCR, Bengaluru BBMP, Hyderabad GHMC, Mumbai MMR, and Pune.
     - *Coastal Erosion*: NCCR-MoES Shoreline Change Atlas (1990 to 2022) monitoring shoreline loss at Sagar Island, Satabhaya, Puducherry, and Chellanam.
     - *Wetland Dynamics*: SAC National Wetland Atlas & Ramsar baseline (1990 to 2020) tracking Chilika Lake, Vembanad-Kol, Deepor Beel, and Sambhar.
     - *Desertification & Land Degradation*: SAC-ISRO Atlas (2003 to 2018) tracking Thar Fringe, Rayalaseema, and Vidarbha scrub encroachment.
     - *Groundwater Stress*: CGWB Dynamic Groundwater Extraction assessments (2004 to 2022) monitoring Punjab Granary, Central Rajasthan, and North Gujarat over-exploitation.
     - *River Course Changes & Avulsion*: Multi-century and decadal fluvial migration (1760 to 2022) including Kosi River avulsion, Majuli Island erosion, and Ganga at Farakka.
   - **Interactive Year Comparison Tool**: Dedicated docked deck allowing users to select Baseline Year (A) and Comparison Year (B), switch between Baseline, Comparison, and Delta Rate ($\Delta$) modes, review net rates of change, and click quick-fly hotspot navigation pills.

6. **National Highways & Expressways (MoRTH / NHAI):**
   - 28 curated corridors: Tolled Expressways (green), National Highway grid (amber), and State Highways/coastal corridors (slate).
   - Route profiles detailing lengths, terminal cities, connecting states, lanes, and toll status.

7. **Natural Disasters & Seismic Risk Intelligence (BIS IS 1893):**
   - Official Bureau of Indian Standards seismic hazard zoning (Zone V, Zone IV, Zone III).
   - Major historical M7+ earthquake epicenters (1819 Kutch, 1897 Assam, 1905 Kangra, 1934 Bihar, 1950 Assam-Tibet, 1993 Latur, 2001 Bhuj, 2004 Tsunami, 2005 Kashmir) with depth, magnitude, and casualty records.

8. **Administrative Boundary Exploration:**
   - All 36 Indian States and Union Territories (ADM1) with official capitals, populations, density, and areas.
   - 735 District administrative boundaries (ADM2) enriched with Census 2011 microdata.
   - State Name Typography Grid directly rendered on map with zoom-responsive scaling.

9. **Animated River Systems & Tributaries:**
   - Dual-layer glowing cyan lines with smooth animated flow vectors.
   - 10 major basins: Ganga, Brahmaputra, Indus, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi, Narmada, Tapi, Sabarmati.
   - Comprehensive hydrological dossiers: length, origin elevation, outflow confluences, riparian states, major dams, and key cities.

10. **Soil Geography Layer (ICAR):**
    - 8-fold ICAR-NBSS&LUP scientific soil classification (Alluvial, Black/Regur, Red & Yellow, Laterite, Arid/Desert, Mountain & Forest).
    - Agricultural suitability profiles, geological genesis, and regional extent.

11. **Demographic Intelligence & Faith Communities:**
    - Fully joined district-level choropleths from Census 2011 PCA and Table C-01:
      - **Population Density** ($persons / km^2$) with plasma color ramp.
      - **Total Population**, **Literacy Rate**, and **Sex Ratio** ($F/1000M$).
      - **6 Faith Communities** (Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain).

12. **State vs State Comparison Studio:**
    - Side-by-side comparative analysis of any two states across demographic, geographic, and religious metrics.

13. **Mobile Touch Responsiveness & Spring-Damped Animations:**
    - 24px–32px invisible hit targets for single-tap selection of fine linear features (roads, rivers, ridges).
    - Proximity-based feature selection (`sortFeaturesByProximity`) to eliminate multi-tap frustration.
    - Smooth CSS Grid accordion animations and rotating chevrons across left and right panels.
